Serenus

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Serenus is a soundscape app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It is made to be private: it collects no personal data, has no accounts, shows no ads, and contains no analytics or tracking of any kind.

What Serenus collects

Nothing. The developer receives no information about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your device

  • Your soundscapes and settings (the sounds you place, the room, your saved mixes and favourites) are stored on your device.
  • Head tracking. With supported AirPods, Serenus reads your head's movement to keep the sounds in place around you. This motion data is used live, on your device, and is never stored or sent anywhere.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Serenus keeps your current mix, saved mixes and favourites the same on your devices using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. This data is stored in your own iCloud account by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy; the developer cannot see it. You can turn this off for Serenus in your device's iCloud settings.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with the developer. These do not identify you, and you can turn this off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Serenus collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

Serenus does not share data with anyone, because it has none to share. Links in the app (such as those in Credits) open in your browser, where the linked website's own policy applies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its effective date.
