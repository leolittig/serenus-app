Serenus

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Serenus doesn't collect any data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer doesn't receive any information about you or how you use the app.

On your device

Your mixes and settings are saved on your device. If you use AirPods, the app reads your head movement to keep the sounds in place. That is only used in the moment and is never saved or sent anywhere.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, your mixes sync between your devices through your own iCloud account. Apple stores this, under Apple's privacy policy, and the developer can't see it. You can turn it off in your iCloud settings.

Crash reports

If you chose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send the developer anonymous crash reports. You can turn this off in Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Serenus doesn't collect data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
