Serenus

Ambient sounds around you, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Serenus plays sounds around you. You place each one where you want it, near or far, and you hear it from there. With AirPods, the sounds stay in place when you turn your head.

Support

If something isn't working or you have an idea, write to leonardolittig@gmail.com.

Questions

Which headphones work with head tracking?

AirPods Pro, AirPods Max and AirPods 3rd generation or later. Other headphones work too, the sounds just don't follow your head.

Does it keep playing with the screen off?

Yes. You can control it from the Lock Screen and Control Center.

Are my mixes on all my devices?

Yes, if you're signed in to iCloud.

Does Serenus collect any data?

No. See the privacy policy.

The recordings, voices and icons Serenus is built from are credited in the app, in Room, About, Credits.
