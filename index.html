Serenus

Soundscapes placed around you, for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Place rain, wind, a creek, birds, a café or a jazz band in the space around you, as near or as far as you like. Every sound is generated live from models of real recordings, so it never loops, and with AirPods the sounds stay where you put them as you turn your head.

Support

Something not working, or an idea? Write to leonardolittig@gmail.com. Please mention your device and which sounds you were playing.

Questions

Which headphones work with head tracking?

AirPods Pro, AirPods Max, AirPods (3rd generation and later) and Beats headphones that support head tracking. Without them, Serenus still places every sound around you; the space just turns with your device instead of your head.

Does Serenus keep playing when the screen is off?

Yes. It plays in the background and from the Lock Screen, and you can control it from the Lock Screen, Control Center and its Live Activity.

Are my mixes on all my devices?

Yes, when you are signed in to iCloud: your current mix, saved mixes and favourites stay in sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Does Serenus collect any data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.

The recordings, voices, bells and icons Serenus is built from are credited in the app, under Room > About > Credits.
